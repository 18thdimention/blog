## Sep 2025 ~ Present

Long-form essays tracing algorithmically generated paths through Seoul.

### Motivation

The flâneur walks without purpose. I wanted to formalize that — to let a random process choose my route and then write faithfully about what I encountered. The mathematical structure of the walk becomes part of the essay's structure.

### Method

For each essay, I choose a stochastic process and a starting point. A Markov chain walk uses transition probabilities based on street connectivity. A Lévy flight produces long jumps interspersed with local wandering. Brownian motion gives the most organic-feeling paths.

I follow the generated route as closely as physically possible, take notes, and write the essay within a week.

### What I learned

Different random processes produce genuinely different experiences of the same city. Lévy flights took me to neighborhoods I would never have visited — industrial edges, hillside cemeteries, wholesale markets at dawn. Brownian motion kept me circling the same few blocks until I noticed details I had always missed.
