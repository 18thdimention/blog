I'm Etienne Jacob, born in 1994 and based in Paris. I'm a software engineer, and in my free time, an artist creating GIF animations through programming. I also enjoy exploring other creative coding projects, including playful interactive installations.

**Contact:** etin.jacob (at) gmail.com

## About my art

Almost all of my animations are created with the constraint of keeping the GIF file size under 5 MB.

I like to think I make art that doesn't need a story or process to be enjoyed, even if it's built on algorithms and math I care about.
